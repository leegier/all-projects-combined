### Change Colors
Edit lines 15-24 in `index.html`:
```css

--neon-purple: #b829f6;    ← Your color here
--neon-pink: #ff0099;      ← Your color here
```

### Change Text
Use Find & Replace in your editor:
- Find: `NYGHTSHADE` → Replace: `Your Product`
- Find: `$5` → Replace: `$9.99`
- Find: Any feature text → Replace: Your text
