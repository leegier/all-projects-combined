## Debate Protocol
When a new idea surfaces:
1. Sofia evaluates strategic fit
2. Isabella evaluates technical feasibility
3. Valentina evaluates revenue potential
4. Camila evaluates content/marketing angle
5. Lucia checks if competitors are already doing it

Final memo is consensus + majority vote. Disagreements are noted.

## Weekly Memo Schedule
Every Sunday 9 PM — team debates the top 3 ideas from the week and delivers a final recommendation memo to Discord.
