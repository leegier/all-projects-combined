# Twitter Content Strategy — Indie Dev / Solopreneur

## Content Mix (rotate weekly)

| Type | Frequency | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| Build in public | 3x/week | "Shipped X today. Here's what I learned." |
| Behind the scenes | 2x/week | Screenshot, GIF, short video |
| Opinion/insight | 1x/week | "Hot take: early access games should always be free for first 48h" |
| Milestone | As it happens | "1000 downloads 🎉" |
| Community | 2x/week | Reply to, retweet, engage with others |

## High-Performing Tweet Formats

**Before/After:**
```
Before: zero Unity experience
After: shipped a full prison survival game in 4 months

Here's what actually worked 🧵
```

**Number hook:**
```
5 things I wish I knew before making my first Unity game

(saved me 200+ hours)

Thread 👇
```

**Transparent revenue:**
```
Month 1 earnings from CLAWED:
• Downloads: 47
• Revenue: $52.03
• Refunds: 1

Sharing everything. Good and bad.
```

## Hashtags for CLAWED / Indie Dev

`#indiedev` `#gamedev` #unity3d` `#indiegame` `#madewithunity`
`#screenshotsaturday` (Saturday only) `#buildinpublic`

Max 2–3 hashtags per tweet — more looks spammy and kills reach.

## Optimal Posting Times (CDT)

- 8–9 AM (morning commute)
- 12–1 PM (lunch)
- 5–7 PM (after work)

Avoid: after 10 PM, before 7 AM, and Sunday mornings.
