# Product Hunt Launch Timeline

## Why Tuesday–Thursday at 12:01 AM PST

- Products launch at midnight PST and compete for 24 hours
- Tue/Wed/Thu have the highest traffic on PH
- Monday is slow (people catching up from weekend)
- Friday/weekend: lower traffic, fewer community votes

## Hour-by-Hour Launch Day Plan

| Time (PST) | Action |
|------------|--------|
| 12:01 AM | Submit product, post maker comment immediately |
| 12:05 AM | Tweet from personal + product accounts |
| 12:15 AM | Message 5-10 close contacts personally |
| 6:00 AM | Check comments, reply to any overnight |
| 8:00 AM | Post update tweet with PH link |
| 9:00 AM | Share in relevant Slack/Discord communities |
| 12:00 PM | Tweet again — "midday update — X upvotes, thanks!" |
| 3:00 PM | Reply to all comments — last push |
| 6:00 PM | Final tweet push before west coast evening |
| 11:59 PM | Screenshot final rank and share |

## What Moves the Needle Most

1. **Personal asks** (DMs, texts) — 10x more effective than broadcast posts
2. **Reply to every comment** — shows active maker, encourages more engagement
3. **Your existing audience** — email list, Twitter followers, Discord members
4. **Timing** (in category) — being first on a slow day beats being 5th on a busy day

## What Doesn't Work

- Mass-posting to every community (seen as spam)
- Asking for upvotes explicitly (against PH rules)
- Launching with no preparation (cold start = lost)
- Launching on a weekend
