## Description
Welcome to Nyghtshade Hollow. A decaying prison where the walls whisper secrets, the air is thick with tension, and every character you meet has a past that shapes how they hunt you.

Built in Unity 6 with URP. Handcrafted systems. Real atmosphere.

**Features:**
- Dossier System: Every inmate has a history that drives their behavior
- Guard AI: Adaptive patrol and response systems
- Stealth & Survival Mechanics: Sneak, hide, manage resources
- Dynamic Lighting & Sound: Environment that presses on your senses
- Prison escape gameplay: Multiple paths, no guaranteed way out
