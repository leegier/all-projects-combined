# Demo Script — 5-Minute Live Demo
## "Done For You OpenClaw Setup"

**Format:** Screen share or in-person. No slides. Show the real thing running.  
**Goal:** Get them to say "I want that." Then book the discovery call.  
**Total time:** 5 minutes hard. Leave room for questions.

---

## OPENING (30 seconds)

> "Okay, so instead of walking you through a deck, I'm just going to show you what it actually looks like. This is a real setup running for a business similar to yours. Watch what happened this morning."

**[Pull up the morning briefing output — email, screenshot, or live terminal]**

> "At 6:04 AM, before [the owner] checked his phone, the system already had his day ready. Let me walk you through what it put together."

---

## THE WALKTHROUGH (2 minutes)

**Step 1 — Morning Briefing**

> "First thing — his calendar. It pulled today's appointments, flagged that the 10 AM call is with a new prospect he hasn't spoken to before, and reminded him there's a follow-up he owed someone from Tuesday."

**Step 2 — Email Triage**

> "His inbox had 34 emails overnight. The system sorted them. Three are flagged urgent — two are from existing clients, one's a new lead. The rest? Newsletters, vendor stuff, nothing that needs him today. He didn't open a single email he didn't need to."

**Step 3 — Meeting Prep**

> "That 10 AM call with the new prospect — he's going in knowing the guy's name, company, what he initially inquired about, and there's a suggested talking point based on the lead source. He walks in looking like he's been thinking about this guy all week."

> "That's what was waiting for him at 6 AM. Before he got out of bed."

**[Pause. Let it land.]**

---

## HANDLING "WHAT IS THIS EXACTLY?" (60 seconds)

*If they ask what the technology is, or how it works:*

> "It's an AI agent we configure specifically for your business — your email, your calendar, your workflow. Think of it like hiring a really efficient assistant who works overnight and never misses anything, except there's no HR paperwork and it costs less than a part-time hire."

> "You don't need to know how it works. You just wake up and it's done. That's the whole point."

*If they push for more technical detail:*

> "We build it on a platform called OpenClaw. It connects to your existing tools — Gmail, Outlook, Google Calendar — and we configure the logic around how your day actually runs. Setup takes about 48 hours once we do the discovery call."

**Keep it short. Don't go deep on tech unless they specifically ask. Most won't.**

---

## HANDLING "HOW MUCH DOES IT COST?" (45 seconds)

> "Setup is $2,500 — that's a one-time fee, covers everything to get it built and running. Then it's $500 a month after that for monitoring, tune-ups, and support."

**Then immediately anchor the value:**

> "Here's the math I usually walk people through: if your time is worth $75 an hour — and honestly for most business owners it's worth more — and this gives you back 10 hours a week, that's $3,000 a month in recovered time. You're ahead by the end of week one."

**Don't apologize for the price. Don't add caveats. State it and let them respond.**

*If they say "that's a lot":*

> "Compared to what? A part-time admin is $2,000–$3,000 a month and they're not working at 6 AM. This runs every day, never calls in sick, and costs $500 a month once it's set up."

---

## THE CLOSE (45 seconds)

> "Look — the demo gives you the idea, but the real value is when it's built around YOUR business. Your clients, your calendar, your inbox, your workflow."

> "What I'd suggest is a 20-minute discovery call. I ask you about how your days run, what's eating your time, and I tell you exactly what the setup would look like for you specifically. No obligation, no hard pitch."

> "After that call, you'll know whether it's a fit. Most people who do the call say yes — but either way you're walking away with a clearer picture of where your time is going."

**[Pull up calendar booking]**

> "I've got openings [Tuesday and Thursday this week]. Which works better for you?"

---

## BACKUP CLOSE (if they stall)

> "What would it take for you to say yes to a 20-minute call? That's all I'm asking for right now."

Or:

> "What's your biggest hesitation? I'd rather address it now than have you wonder about it."

---

## POST-DEMO NOTES

- **Never end with "let me know."** Always end with a specific ask.
- **Send a follow-up within 1 hour** — "Great talking with you. Here's the link to book: [calendly]"
- **If they go cold after the demo:** One follow-up 3 days later: "Still thinking about it? Happy to answer any questions or show you a different piece of the setup."
