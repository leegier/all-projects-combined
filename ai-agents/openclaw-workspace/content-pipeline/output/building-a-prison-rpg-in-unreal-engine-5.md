# Building a Prison RPG in Unreal Engine 5

The Victorian Gothic style of architecture is characterized by its use of pointed arches, ribbed vaults, and ornate decorations. This style was particularly popular during the Victorian era (1837–1901) and can be seen in many buildings and structures from this time period.

In the context of game design, a Victorian Gothic-inspired prison would feature grand, imposing architecture with intricate details and ornamentation. The prison itself would likely be divided into different sections or wings, each serving a specific purpose.

### Assets

To create such an environment in Unreal Engine 5, we need to ensure that our assets are consistent with the Victorian Gothic style. This includes buildings, furniture, lighting, and other environmental elements. We can achieve this by using a combination of 3D modeling software and texturing techniques.
