## 0. The #1 blocker: render-pipeline conversion

Active pipeline is **HDRP**, but the packs span every pipeline. Most render **magenta** until their materials are converted.

| Pipeline | Count | Action |
|---|---|---|
| HDRP-ready | 9 | Use as-is |
| Built-in/Standard | 34 | `Edit ▸ Rendering ▸ Convert Built-in Materials to HDRP` (mostly automatic) |
| URP | 7 | Manual re-shader (the upgrader does NOT do URP→HDRP) |
| Mixed | 8 | Case-by-case |
| Empty/audio/config | 3 | Skip |

Water packs (AQUAS-Lite, MobileWaterShader) are built-in and break under HDRP → use Unity 6's **native HDRP Water** instead.
