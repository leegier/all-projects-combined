### Cover Letter 1 — Customer Support / Ticket Automation

Subject: AI customer support automation — I've built this before

Automating first-line support responses is one of the highest-ROI AI applications I build, and I can scope and build yours quickly.

Here's the typical architecture:
1. New support ticket arrives (email, Zendesk webhook, Intercom, or custom form)
2. AI reads the ticket, classifies it (billing, technical, general inquiry, etc.)
3. For simple/known issues — AI drafts a complete response and either sends it automatically or queues it for one-click approval
4. For complex issues — AI writes a summary + suggested next steps and routes to the right team member
5. Everything logged to a dashboard so you can review quality and track resolution rates

This setup typically handles 60-80% of incoming tickets without human intervention. Your team focuses on the 20% that actually needs them.
