# 🎬 Demo Video Script — 60-Second Screen Recording
**"Peak Roofing Morning Briefing" — Sales Collateral**

---

## Pre-Recording Setup
- Open `demo-briefing.md` in a clean browser tab or rendered markdown viewer
- Font size bumped up (readable on video)
- Phone notifications silenced
- Clean desktop — no clutter visible

---

## THE SCRIPT

---

**[0:00–0:05] — HOOK. Camera on screen. Cursor idle.**

> *"It's 7 AM. Mike owns a roofing company. Before he finishes his first cup of coffee — his AI agent has already done this."*

*[Scroll slowly from the top of the briefing as you say it.]*

---

**[0:05–0:12] — Weather section highlighted**

> *"Weather check. Good conditions today — both crews can roll. No calls to make, no guessing."*

*[Hover over the weather block. Let it sit for 2 seconds.]*

---

**[0:12–0:24] — Scroll to Emails section**

> *"Three emails in his inbox this morning. His agent already read them and pulled out what matters."*

*[Pause on the Jennifer Harmon lead.]*

> *"A storm damage lead just came in — potential $10,000 job. Insurance already filed. She wants an estimate this week."*

*[Pause on Darnell's message.]*

> *"His foreman texted at 11 PM asking about Saturday hours. Needs an answer by 7:30 AM. It's flagged. Mike sees it the second he wakes up."*

---

**[0:24–0:34] — Scroll to Job Sites table**

> *"Two active sites today. Addresses, crew names, where each job stands. One glance and Mike knows exactly what's happening in the field."*

*[Let the table sit on screen for 3 full seconds.]*

---

**[0:34–0:42] — Scroll to Urgent + Competitor sections**

> *"Here's the part that saves him real money."*

*[Pause on the permit expiration.]*

> *"A permit expires in 6 days. Phone number already there. One call, problem solved."*

*[Scroll to competitor intel.]*

> *"And his top competitor is running Facebook ads this week targeting his exact territory. Now Mike knows before he starts his day — not after he loses the job."*

---

**[0:42–0:52] — Scroll to Action Items**

> *"Bottom of the briefing — four action items, ranked by urgency. Reply to the foreman, call the lead, schedule the inspection, lock the shingle price."*

*[Let the list sit. Slow scroll.]*

> *"No spreadsheet. No checking five apps. Just — what do I do today."*

---

**[0:52–1:00] — Cut to face or stay on screen**

> *"This lands in Mike's phone at 7 AM every single morning. Takes him 90 seconds to read. Saves him hours of chasing information."*

> *"I built this for roofing companies. Want me to set yours up?"*

*[End on briefing — let it linger 2 seconds before fade.]*

---

## Production Notes

- **Total runtime target:** 58–62 seconds
- **Tone:** Calm, confident. Not salesy. Let the product speak.
- **Music (optional):** Low ambient background, -20dB under voice
- **Caption overlay (optional):** Key stats as text — "$10K lead", "6 days to permit expiry", "competitor ad detected"
- **Best platform:** LinkedIn video post or loom.com embed in proposal emails
- **CTA overlay at end:** "DM me 'BRIEFING' to see your custom version"
