# Top 5 Niches — "Done For You OpenClaw Setup"
## Ranked by: Ease of Sale × Ability to Pay

---

## Rank #1 — Roofing Contractors

**Ease of sale: 9/10 | Ability to pay: 8/10**

### What They Struggle With
Owner-operators running 3–8 crews. Their mornings are chaos: insurance adjusters blowing up the phone, material suppliers confirming orders, crews texting about site issues — all before 7 AM. They're the bottleneck for every decision, and email is a graveyard of stuff they meant to respond to.

### What the Agent Solves
- Morning summary of open insurance claims, pending approvals, and urgent follow-ups
- Flags any new lead inquiries before the day starts
- Preps them for any adjuster calls with job notes attached
- Triage so they're not wading through 40 emails to find the 3 that matter

### What to Charge
- $2,500 setup / $500 month — standard
- Upsell: Add lead intake triage for +$250/month (flags new estimate requests immediately)

### Sample Pitch Line
> "You're the first one up and the last one off the phone every day. What if your morning was already sorted before you pulled up to the first job site?"

## Rank #2 — Real Estate Agents (Solo & Small Teams)

**Ease of sale: 8/10 | Ability to pay: 9/10**

### What They Struggle With
Leads go cold because agents can't keep up. They're juggling active listings, buyer showings, lender emails, and follow-ups — and hot leads fall through the cracks because there's no system. Top producers feel it least; mid-tier agents feel it most and have the most to gain.

### What the Agent Solves
- Morning lead prioritization: who reached out, how hot they are, last touchpoint
- Flags anyone who's been waiting >48 hours for a response
- Preps them for showing appointments (client history, notes, what they're looking for)
- Inbox triage cuts through MLS alerts and admin noise to surface real conversations

### What to Charge
- $2,500 setup / $500 month — standard
- Works especially well for agents doing $3M+ GCI who value their time clearly

### Sample Pitch Line
> "Every lead you don't follow up with in 24 hours is money walking out the door. What if the follow-up list was waiting for you every morning before you even checked your phone?"

## Rank #3 — General Contractors (Residential Remodeling)

**Ease of sale: 7/10 | Ability to pay: 8/10**

### What They Struggle With
Managing multiple active projects while still being the person clients call for everything. They hate being CC'd on every email but also can't afford to miss anything important. The owner spends 2–3 hours a day on operational noise that shouldn't require their attention at all.

### What the Agent Solves
- Project status digest every morning: what's on schedule, what's slipping, what needs a decision
- Flags client emails that actually need a response (vs. FYI chains)
- Subcontractor check-in summaries so they're not chasing status manually
- Meeting prep for walkthroughs — pulls relevant project notes automatically

### What to Charge
- $2,500 setup / $500 month — standard
- For larger GCs (5+ active projects): $3,500 setup — justify with complexity
- Retainer upsell: project status board integration for +$300/month

### Sample Pitch Line
> "You're running 4 jobs and you're still the one answering every email. What if you woke up knowing exactly which of those 4 jobs needs your attention today — and it wasn't all of them?"

## Rank #4 — Insurance Agents (Independent)

**Ease of sale: 7/10 | Ability to pay: 7/10**

### What They Struggle With
Renewal season is a massacre. Hundreds of policy renewals, claims inquiries, and new lead follow-ups all competing for attention. They're good at selling but terrible at the admin stack that surrounds it. Most are solo or running a 2–3 person shop with no real ops person.

### What the Agent Solves
- Daily renewal reminder digest with client contact info attached
- New lead prioritization — who came in, what they need, recommended next step
- Flags any claims-related emails for immediate attention
- Morning briefing includes policy anniversary dates coming up in the next 30 days

### What to Charge
- $2,500 setup / $500 month — standard
- Slightly lower conversion than roofing/real estate (more skeptical of tech) but higher lifetime value once they're in

### Sample Pitch Line
> "Renewal season shouldn't feel like triage. What if you started every morning already knowing which clients to call and why?"

## Rank #5 — HVAC / Plumbing / Electrical Service Businesses

**Ease of sale: 6/10 | Ability to pay: 7/10**

### What They Struggle With
Dispatch is a mess. The owner is also the estimator, the one who handles upset customers, and the one approving invoices. Emergency calls blow up the schedule. They're reactive by nature — the business runs them instead of the other way around.

### What the Agent Solves
- Morning job board summary: what's scheduled, who's confirmed, what's still open
- Flags any customer complaint emails or bad review alerts immediately
- Unanswered estimate requests surfaced before the crew hits the road
- Follow-up queue for estimates that haven't been accepted in 3+ days

### What to Charge
- $2,000 setup / $400 month — slightly lower (smaller avg revenue per owner vs. roofing)
- Strong upsell: add Google review monitoring + response drafting for +$150/month

### Sample Pitch Line
> "You've got 8 techs on the road and you're still the one answering the phone for upset customers. What if the stuff that actually needs you was already sorted by the time your first truck rolled out?"

## Targeting Priority

Go **roofing first** — fastest yes cycle, highest pain, least skepticism about automation. They're used to paying for systems that make their crews more efficient. This is no different.

Go **real estate second** — higher average income, but more decision-making paralysis. Needs slightly more social proof before they sign.

**Avoid:** Law firms, medical practices, accountants (compliance concerns slow everything down). Save them for v2 once you have case studies.
