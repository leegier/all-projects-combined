# Cold Outreach Scripts — "Done For You OpenClaw Setup"

Target: Local business owners — roofing, real estate, contractors
Angle: Sell the OUTCOME. Never mention AI, tech, or software first.

---

## 1. LinkedIn DM (under 150 words)

**Subject line (if InMail): "10 hours back per week"**

---

Hey [First Name] —

Noticed you're running [Company Name]. Quick question: how much time do you personally spend every morning sorting through emails, prepping for calls, and figuring out what actually needs your attention today?

For most contractors/roofers/agents I talk to, it's 1.5–2 hours a day. Every day.

I help business owners in [city] get that time back — without hiring anyone. Your day is laid out for you before 6 AM. Inbox sorted. Meetings prepped. Done.

Takes 48 hours to set up. Costs less than one employee's first week.

Worth a 20-minute call to see it live?

— [Your Name]

---

*Word count: ~110. Adjust "[contractors/roofers/agents]" to match the specific niche.*

## Notes on Targeting

**Roofers:**
- Pain: Mornings destroyed by insurance adjusters, supplier calls, crew check-ins
- Hook: "Before you're on your first roof of the day, you already know exactly what needs your attention"

**Real Estate Agents:**
- Pain: Leads going cold because they can't keep up with follow-ups
- Hook: "Your hottest leads flagged every morning before you've had coffee"

**General Contractors:**
- Pain: Managing 5 subcontractors and 3 projects while still being the one who answers emails
- Hook: "You stop being the bottleneck"

**Rule:** Always lead with time saved or money recovered. Never lead with the technology.
