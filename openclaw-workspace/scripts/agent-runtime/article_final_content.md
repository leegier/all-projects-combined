# Building a Prison RPG in Unreal Engine 5

## Introduction
The prison RPG, a genre that has seen its fair share of games, is one that holds a special place in the hearts of many game developers. With the advent of Unreal Engine 5 and its numerous features, such as Nanite virtualized geometry and Lumen global illumination, it's the perfect time to tackle this genre.

## Designing The Hollow Penitentiary
The game takes place in The Hollow Penitentiary, a Victorian Gothic style prison built on the principles of reform and rehabilitation. However, this is not your average prison, as it holds dark secrets and mysteries waiting to be uncovered. The player takes on the role of an inmate, tasked with uncovering the truth behind the prison's sinister past.

## Art Style
The game's art style is heavily influenced by the Victorian Gothic era, characterized by intricate details, ornate decorations, and a sense of foreboding. Every aspect of the game, from the architecture to the character designs, has been carefully crafted to create an immersive experience that transports the player into the world of The Hollow Penitentiary.

## 50K+ Assets in UE5.7
With the power of Unreal Engine 5 and its numerous features, such as the new physics-based rendering engine, it's easy to see how a game like this could come to life with 50K+ assets. The team worked tirelessly to create a richly detailed world that feels alive, with every aspect of the environment, from the walls to the furniture, meticulously crafted to create an atmosphere of unease and tension.

## Conclusion
Building a Prison RPG in Unreal Engine 5 is no easy task, but the end result is well worth it. With its intricate design, Victorian Gothic art style, and 50K+ assets, this game promises to be one for the ages. The combination of UE5's features and the team's dedication has resulted in a game that truly shines, and one that will leave players with a lasting impression.
