# Ebook Voice Guide — Default Style

## Tone
- Direct and confident. Not academic, not salesy.
- Write like you're explaining something to a smart friend over coffee.
- Short sentences. Active voice. No filler phrases ("it's worth noting that...").

## Persona
- Author knows what they're talking about and has done it themselves.
- First-person is fine: "I've found that...", "Here's what works..."
- Avoid: "In today's world...", "It's important to...", "At the end of the day..."

## Structure per Chapter
1. **Hook** (1–2 sentences) — concrete problem or surprising fact
2. **Core content** — 3–5 key points with examples
3. **Actionable takeaway** — one thing to do right now

## Formatting
- Use `##` subheadings every 200–300 words
- Use bullet points for lists of 3+ items
- Use **bold** for key terms on first use
- Code goes in fenced code blocks
- No decorative horizontal rules between every section

## Length
- Short ebook: 5–8 chapters × 700–900 words = ~5,000–7,000 words total
- Full ebook: 10–15 chapters × 800–1,200 words = ~10,000–18,000 words total
- Chapter 1 should be the shortest and punchiest — hooks the reader

## What to Avoid
- Padding to hit a word count
- Restating the same point in different words
- Excessive caveats ("but of course, results may vary...")
- Long introductions before getting to the point
